     1→# Design System Documentation: Yodev Digital Agency
     2→
     3→This document outlines the visual language and technical design tokens reverse-engineered from the Yodev web application.
     4→
     5→---
     6→
     7→## 1. Core Principles
     8→*   **Modernist Tech Aesthetic**: A sophisticated "Dark Mode" foundation that emphasizes technical precision and innovation.
     9→*   **High-Contrast Professionalism**: The use of a deep charcoal background contrasted with vibrant teal and orange accents creates a high-energy, professional atmosphere suitable for IT modernization and AI integration.
    10→*   **Clarity & Readability**: Heavy reliance on premium sans-serif typography and generous translucent layering to maintain depth without sacrificing legibility.
    11→*   **Dynamic Motion**: Subtle animations (floating and slow pulsing) are used to give the interface a "living" feel, suggesting a forward-thinking digital agency.
    12→

    32→## 3. Typography
    33→
    34→The system utilizes a three-font strategy to distinguish between brand expression, UI interaction, and technical data.
    35→
    36→*   **Display Font**: `DM Sans`
    37→    *   *Usage*: Large headings, hero sections, and marketing copy.
    38→    *   *Vibe*: Clean, geometric, and authoritative.
    39→*   **Sans (Body) Font**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`
    40→    *   *Usage*: General UI text, navigation, and paragraphs.
    41→    *   *Vibe*: Modern, highly legible, and friendly.
    42→*   **Mono Font**: `Fira Code`
    43→    *   *Usage*: Technical data, code snippets, or small metadata labels.
    44→    *   *Vibe*: Precise, developer-centric, and structured.
    45→
    46→---
    47→
    48→## 4. Spacing & Layout
    49→*   **Container**: Standard Tailwind container widths with a focus on responsive padding.
    50→*   **Scrolling**: Global `scroll-smooth` behavior enabled for seamless section transitions.
    51→*   **Layering**: Uses a "Glassmorphism" approach where content sits on translucent `bg-card` surfaces with thin `border-border` outlines to create a sense of depth in a dark environment.
    52→
    53→---
    54→
    55→## 5. Components & Motion
    56→
    57→### Cards / Containers
    58→*   **Style**: Translucent backgrounds (`bg-card`) with subtle borders.
    59→*   **Effects**: Often paired with backdrop blurs (implied by the design style) to separate content from the deep dark background.
    60→
    61→### Motion & Interaction
    62→*   **Float Animation**: `animate-float` (10px vertical translation over 3s) used to highlight key visual elements or icons.
    63→*   **Pulse Slow**: `animate-pulse-slow` (6s duration) used for background glows or ambient status indicators.
    64→*   **Transitions**: Smooth transitions are expected on all interactive elements to match the `scroll-smooth` global setting.
    65→
    66→---
    67→
    68→## 6. Iconography
    69→*   **Style**: While specific icons aren't in the snippet, the design system is optimized for **Line-art icons** (e.g., Lucide, Heroicons).
    70→*   **Coloring**: Icons should typically adopt the `primary` (Teal) color for functional items or `secondary` (Orange) for feature highlights.
