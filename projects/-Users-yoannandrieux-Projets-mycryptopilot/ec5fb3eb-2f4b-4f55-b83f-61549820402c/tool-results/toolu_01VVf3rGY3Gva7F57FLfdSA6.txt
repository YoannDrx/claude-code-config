     3→# Design System Documentation: MyCryptoPilot
     4→
     5→## 1. Core Principles
     6→The design system follows a **"Cyber-Fintech Minimalist"** aesthetic. It combines the high-contrast, dark-mode look of modern trading platforms with sophisticated "Glassmorphism" and tactile textures.
     7→
     8→*   **Risk-First Intelligence**: The UI uses high-contrast accents (Emerald vs. Crimson) to immediately communicate market sentiment and risk.
     9→*   **Depth & Texture**: Uses layered backgrounds (Mesh gradients + SVG Grain) to create a premium, "physical" digital space.
    10→*   **Precision & Clarity**: High-end typography (Sora) and generous whitespace emphasize data accuracy and professional-grade tools.

    36→## 3. Typography
    37→The system uses **Sora**, a geometric sans-serif designed for clarity in digital interfaces.
    38→
    39→*   **Primary Font**: `Sora, sans-serif`
    40→*   **Weights**: 
    41→    *   `300` (Light): Secondary descriptions.
    42→    *   `400` (Regular): Body text.
    43→    *   `600` (Semi-bold): Sub-headings and buttons.
    44→    *   `800` (Extra-bold): Hero titles and branding.
    45→*   **Scale**:
    46→    *   **Hero Title**: Likely `text-5xl` to `text-7xl` with `font-extrabold`.
    47→    *   **Body**: `text-base` (16px) with `leading-relaxed`.
    48→
    49→---
    50→
    51→## 4. Spacing & Layout
    52→*   **Container**: Standardized centered layouts with `max-width` (likely `max-w-7xl`).
    53→*   **Layout Engine**: Flexbox and CSS Grid are used for structural alignment.
    54→*   **Layering Strategy (Z-Index)**:
    55→    *   `z-[-1]`: Mesh Gradients (Background depth).
    56→    *   `z-[0]`: Main Content.
    57→    *   `z-[50]`: Grain Texture (Overlay for tactile feel).
    58→*   **Scrolling**: Smooth scrolling enabled via `scroll-smooth`.
    59→
    60→---
    61→
    62→## 5. Components
    63→
    64→### 5.1 The "Hyper-Border" Container
    65→A signature component used for cards and sections.
    66→*   **Style**: Semi-transparent background with a `glass-border`.
    67→*   **Effect**: Likely utilizes `backdrop-filter: blur()` to create a frosted glass effect over the mesh gradients.
    68→
    69→### 5.2 Background System
    70→The background is a composite of three layers:
    71→1.  **Base Layer**: Solid `#05070a`.
    72→2.  **Atmospheric Layer**: `.mesh-gradient` using radial gradients of Emerald and Crimson at low opacity.
    73→3.  **Texture Layer**: `.grain` using a fractal noise SVG filter at `0.04` opacity to prevent "banding" in gradients and add a premium feel.
    74→

    76→
    77→## 6. Iconography
    78→*   **Style**: Thin-stroke, geometric icons.
    79→*   **Implementation**: Likely Lucide or Phosphor Icons to match the `Sora` typeface's geometric nature.
    80→*   **Coloring**: Icons should follow the accent palette (Emerald for features, Crimson for risk alerts).
