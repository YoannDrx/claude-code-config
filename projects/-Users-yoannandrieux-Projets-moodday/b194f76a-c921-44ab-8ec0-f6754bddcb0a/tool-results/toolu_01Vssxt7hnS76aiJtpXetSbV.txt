     3→# Design System: Moodday
     4→
     5→## 1. Core Principles
     6→*   **Soothing & Organic:** The use of "Warm" off-whites instead of pure whites, combined with teal and sage greens, creates a calming, therapeutic atmosphere suitable for mental health.
     7→*   **Soft Modernism:** High border radii (`2xl`) and custom "soft" shadows create a friendly, approachable, and non-aggressive user interface.
     8→*   **Clarity & Focus:** The choice of the Inter typeface ensures high legibility, while the "scroll-smooth" behavior suggests a seamless, low-friction user experience.
