    59→## Sequence of Instructions (Do not deviate, skip, or optimize)
    60→
    61→### 1. Begin User Discovery
    62→
    63→**Opening Exploration:**
    64→"Now that we understand what {{project_name}} does, let's define who it's for.
    65→
    66→**User Discovery:**
    67→
    68→- Who experiences the problem we're solving?
    69→- Are there different types of users with different needs?
    70→- Who gets the most value from this solution?
    71→- Are there primary users and secondary users we should consider?
    72→
    73→Let's start by identifying the main user groups."
    74→
    75→### 2. Primary User Segment Development
    76→
    77→**Persona Development Process:**
    78→For each primary user segment, create rich personas:
    79→
    80→**Name & Context:**
    81→
    82→- Give them a realistic name and brief backstory
    83→- Define their role, environment, and context
    84→- What motivates them? What are their goals?
    85→
    86→**Problem Experience:**
    87→
    88→- How do they currently experience the problem?
    89→- What workarounds are they using?
    90→- What are the emotional and practical impacts?
    91→
    92→**Success Vision:**
    93→
    94→- What would success look like for them?
    95→- What would make them say "this is exactly what I needed"?
    96→
    97→**Primary User Questions:**
    98→
    99→- "Tell me about a typical person who would use {{project_name}}"
   100→- "What's their day like? Where does our product fit in?"
   101→- "What are they trying to accomplish that's hard right now?"
   102→
   103→### 3. Secondary User Segment Exploration
   104→
   105→**Secondary User Considerations:**
   106→
   107→- "Who else benefits from this solution, even if they're not the primary user?"
   108→- "Are there admin, support, or oversight roles we should consider?"
   109→- "Who influences the decision to adopt or purchase this product?"
   110→- "Are there partner or stakeholder users who matter?"
   111→
   112→### 4. User Journey Mapping
   113→
   114→**Journey Elements:**
   115→Map key interactions for each user segment:
   116→
   117→- **Discovery:** How do they find out about the solution?
   118→- **Onboarding:** What's their first experience like?
   119→- **Core Usage:** How do they use the product day-to-day?
   120→- **Success Moment:** When do they realize the value?
   121→- **Long-term:** How does it become part of their routine?
   122→
   123→**Journey Questions:**
   124→
   125→- "Walk me through how [Persona Name] would discover and start using {{project_name}}"
   126→- "What's their 'aha!' moment?"
   127→- "How does this product change how they work or live?"
   128→
