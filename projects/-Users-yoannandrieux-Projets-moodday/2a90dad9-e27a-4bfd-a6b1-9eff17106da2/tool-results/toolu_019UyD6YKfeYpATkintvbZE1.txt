    59→## Sequence of Instructions (Do not deviate, skip, or optimize)
    60→
    61→### 1. Begin Success Metrics Discovery
    62→
    63→**Opening Exploration:**
    64→"Now that we know who {{project_name}} serves and what problem it solves, let's define what success looks like.
    65→
    66→**Success Discovery:**
    67→
    68→- How will we know we're succeeding for our users?
    69→- What would make users say 'this was worth it'?
    70→- What metrics show we're creating real value?
    71→
    72→Let's start with the user perspective."
    73→
    74→### 2. User Success Metrics
    75→
    76→**User Success Questions:**
    77→Define success from the user's perspective:
    78→
    79→- "What outcome are users trying to achieve?"
    80→- "How will they know the product is working for them?"
    81→- "What's the moment where they realize this is solving their problem?"
    82→- "What behaviors indicate users are getting value?"
    83→
    84→**User Success Exploration:**
    85→Guide from vague to specific metrics:
    86→
    87→- "Users are happy" → "Users complete [key action] within [timeframe]"
    88→- "Product is useful" → "Users return [frequency] and use [core feature]"
    89→- Focus on outcomes and behaviors, not just satisfaction scores
    90→
    91→### 3. Business Objectives
    92→
    93→**Business Success Questions:**
    94→Define business success metrics:
    95→
    96→- "What does success look like for the business at 3 months? 12 months?"
    97→- "Are we measuring revenue, user growth, engagement, something else?"
    98→- "What business metrics would make you say 'this is working'?"
    99→- "How does this product contribute to broader company goals?"
   100→
   101→**Business Success Categories:**
   102→
   103→- **Growth Metrics:** User acquisition, market penetration
   104→- **Engagement Metrics:** Usage patterns, retention, satisfaction
   105→- **Financial Metrics:** Revenue, profitability, cost efficiency
   106→- **Strategic Metrics:** Market position, competitive advantage
   107→
   108→### 4. Key Performance Indicators
   109→
   110→**KPI Development Process:**
   111→Define specific, measurable KPIs:
   112→
   113→- Transform objectives into measurable indicators
   114→- Ensure each KPI has a clear measurement method
   115→- Define targets and timeframes where appropriate
   116→- Include leading indicators that predict success
   117→
   118→**KPI Examples:**
   119→
   120→- User acquisition: "X new users per month"
   121→- Engagement: "Y% of users complete core journey weekly"
   122→- Business impact: "$Z in cost savings or revenue generation"
   123→
   124→### 5. Connect Metrics to Strategy
   125→
   126→**Strategic Alignment:**
   127→Ensure metrics align with product vision and user needs:
   128→
   129→- Connect each metric back to the product vision
   130→- Ensure user success metrics drive business success
   131→- Validate that metrics measure what truly matters
   132→- Avoid vanity metrics that don't drive decisions
